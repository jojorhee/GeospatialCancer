Student-Led Research and Advocacy

Investigating Cancer Patterns Across Texas

Texas Cancer Advocacy Project is a student-led initiative that combines data analysis and civic advocacy to investigate cancer patterns across Texas and examine possible environmental and industrial factors that may deserve further research. This project does not automatically assume casuation, and is still in progress.
