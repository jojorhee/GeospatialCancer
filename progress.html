Progress

Identified Carcinogenic Site

We have identified one potentially carcinogenic industrial site:

  • KSolv Maritime and Petroleum Plant (Channelview, Texas)
    • Multiple sources — including local news articles, legal reports, and research — document excess benzene emissions.
    • These emissions correlate with elevated leukemia incidence rates in nearby communities.

Technical Development

We built a web application that visualizes cancer data across the entire state of Texas.

  • Data input is ongoing.
  • We plan to expand to other states once we achieve measurable impact in Texas.

Environmental Research Progress

Our team has identified dozens of potentially carcinogenic sites across Texas based on emission records, cancer‑rate anomalies, and environmental reports.

Advocacy Progress

As part of our remediation strategy, we have begun engaging in political outreach:

  • Researching U.S. and Texas representatives
  • Reviewing their environmental positions and committee roles
  • Conducting interviews with several representatives (with more scheduled)
  • Sharing findings to encourage regulatory action on carcinogen‑emitting facilities
