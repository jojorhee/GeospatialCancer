Mission

Who We Are

We are a student‑driven legislative advocacy and data analysis project focused on uncovering environmental carcinogen sources across Texas. Our work integrates computer programming, cancer statistics, social research, and environmental data analysis.

What We Do

We investigate industrial sites that release carcinogens — especially benzene — into surrounding communities. These emissions correlate with elevated cancer rates, including leukemia, in multiple Texas regions.

Why This Matters

Communities often lack access to clear information about carcinogenic exposure. By combining technical research with civic engagement, we aim to push for stronger protections and regulatory action.

Our Advocacy Role

As part of our mission, we research local, state, and federal representatives and communicate our findings to them. This includes reaching out to offices, sharing data, and conducting interviews to raise awareness about carcinogenic industrial activity.
